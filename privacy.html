Privacy

What we collect

When you book, Square collects the booker’s name and email, the lesson chosen, the appointment time and payment details. Payment card details are handled by Square and never reach this site. For actors under 18, the parent or guardian books and gives consent.

How it’s used

Only to schedule and deliver lessons and to keep the records required for payments and refunds. We don’t sell personal information and don’t use it for advertising.

Young actors

Lessons for actors under 18 are booked by a parent or legal guardian. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Who else handles it

Square handles booking and payment.

How long we keep it

Booking records are kept as long as needed for accounting.
