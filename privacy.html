What we collect

When you book, we collect what's needed to schedule and run the lesson: the booker's name and email, the lesson chosen, the appointment time and payment details. Payment card details are handled by Square and never reach this site. For actors under 18, we also collect the parent or guardian's name and email, the actor's first name and age, and the guardian's consent.

If you send a take, we receive the recording and the contact details on the form.

How it's used

Only to schedule, deliver and follow up on lessons and feedback, and to keep records required for payments and refunds. We don't sell personal information and don't use it for advertising.

Young actors

Lessons and submissions for actors under 18 are arranged by a parent or legal guardian. We don't knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. Recordings of minors are kept private, used only for feedback to that actor, and never published.
