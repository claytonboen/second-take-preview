The lesson

  • One-to-one with Clayton. A private lesson, not a group class.
  • Your material. Bring an audition, a self-tape, or a scene you’re working on. Or use one of his free sides.
  • Direction, then another take. You perform the scene, Clayton gives you a direction, you run it again.
  • Kids and teens. A parent or guardian books the lesson and gives consent.
