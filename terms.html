The lessons

Lessons are one-to-one acting instruction with Clayton Boen. They’re educational. No lesson guarantees an audition, a booking, representation or any particular result.

Booking and payment

Lessons are booked and paid in advance through Square. Prices are shown before checkout.

Actors under 18

A parent or legal guardian must book, give consent, and be present or nearby during the lesson. The guardian is responsible for the minor’s participation.

Recording

Lessons aren’t recorded unless everyone agrees beforehand, and for minors that includes the guardian.

Conduct

Lessons are a respectful, professional space. A lesson can be ended if that’s not the case.

Materials

The free sides are Clayton Boen’s original writing. You may download and perform them for practice, class, auditions and self-tapes. Please don’t republish or sell them.
